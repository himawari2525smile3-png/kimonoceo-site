PROFILE

Makoto Iimura

Makoto Iimura (飯村惇) is the representative member of HiMaWaRi LLC (HiMaWaRi合同会社), a management advisory firm in Nakameguro, Tokyo. He works with owners of small and mid-sized companies in Japan whose decisions all pass through one person, and helps them put the criteria behind those decisions into words so that their teams, and AI, can carry them.

What he does

Most of the owners he works with run profitable companies of around 10 to 50 employees. The business has grown, but every judgment call still lands on the owner's desk. Hiring a "right-hand person" or buying an AI tool rarely fixes this on its own, because the owner's way of deciding has never been written down.

His work starts there. He sits in on real decisions, writes down how the owner decides, and then moves those criteria to the people and the AI systems that will use them. The engagements include:

  • Sitting in on management decisions as a dedicated advisor
  • Turning the owner's decision criteria into written rules that staff and AI can follow
  • Building an AI "second self" of the owner for routine judgments
  • Redesigning which roles are handled by people and which by AI

He also uses "36 Types", a personality framework developed in-house, to describe each owner's decision-making habits.
